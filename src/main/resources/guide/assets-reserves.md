# Assets and Reserves

Document assets with the two most recent monthly statements for every account used for the down payment, closing costs or reserves.

Reserves are measured in months of the full housing payment. Conforming loans need two months, jumbo loans up to 1.5 million dollars need six months, and larger jumbo loans need twelve months.

Any single deposit above 50 percent of the borrower's gross monthly income needs a sourcing letter and documentation of where the funds came from.

Gift funds need a signed gift letter and proof of transfer. Gifts may not be used for reserves on jumbo loans.
