# Credit Standards

The desk requires a minimum representative credit score of 680 for conforming loans and 720 for jumbo loans. The representative score is the middle of three bureau scores, or the lower of two.

Each borrower must show at least three tradelines with twelve months of history, one of them open in the last six months. Authorized user accounts do not count toward the tradeline minimum.

A credit report must be no more than 120 days old on the note date. Order a new report if the file will close after that window.

Any mortgage payment more than 30 days late in the last twelve months makes the file ineligible unless the desk lead approves an exception.
