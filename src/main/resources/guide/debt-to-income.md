# Debt-to-Income Limits

The maximum debt-to-income ratio is 43 percent for conforming loans. The desk may allow up to 45 percent when the borrower holds at least twelve months of reserves after closing.

Jumbo loans carry a maximum debt-to-income ratio of 40 percent, with no compensating factor exceptions.

Count student loan payments at the amount on the credit report, or at 1 percent of the balance when the report shows no payment.

Exclude installment debts with ten or fewer payments left, unless the payment exceeds 5 percent of gross monthly income.
