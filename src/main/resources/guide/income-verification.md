# Income and Employment Verification

Salaried borrowers document income with W-2 forms for the last two years and pay stubs covering the most recent 30 days.

Self-employed borrowers provide two years of signed personal and business tax returns, plus a year-to-date profit and loss statement. Income that declined more than 20 percent year over year needs a written explanation.

Complete a verbal verification of employment within ten business days of the note date. For self-employed borrowers, verify the business through a CPA letter or a state business registry instead.

Bonus, overtime and commission income count only with a two-year history, and the desk averages them over that period.
