# Policy Exceptions

The desk lead approves all exceptions to these guidelines. Submit an exception request with the loan number, the guideline at issue and the compensating factors.

Acceptable compensating factors include reserves well above the minimum, a debt-to-income ratio at least 5 points below the limit, and a credit score at least 40 points above the minimum.

The desk answers exception requests within two business days. An approved exception is valid for the life of the loan file, and expires if the file is withdrawn.

Exceptions to the jumbo debt-to-income limit, or to the minimum credit score, are never granted.
