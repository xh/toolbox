# Property and Appraisal

An appraisal stays valid for 120 days from its effective date. An update with a recertification of value extends it once, by up to 60 days.

Loans above 2 million dollars need a second full appraisal, and the desk uses the lower of the two values.

Condominium units must be in a warrantable project. Non-warrantable condos go to the desk lead as a policy exception.

Properties with more than ten acres, or with agricultural income, are not eligible for desk programs.
